About Noorani

Cooked over coal, the way it's meant to be.

Noorani Restaurant is a neighbourhood fixture on Rashid Minhas Road — a spot where the tandoor never really cools down. Every order of tikka, karahi and biryani is cooked to order, not held under a heat lamp.

We keep the menu focused on what we do well: BBQ, karahi and rice dishes, alongside quick options like burgers and sandwiches for the days you want something faster.
